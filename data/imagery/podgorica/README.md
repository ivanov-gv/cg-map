# Podgorica orthophoto sample

Source: eMapa Ortofoto 2025, downloaded 18 October 2026 at the project owner's request. Original tile URLs, coordinates and SHA-256 hashes are in manifest.json. The WebP preview is a compressed mosaic of those 16 tiles; original tile bytes are preserved.

Coverage: approximately 900 x 900 metres around central Podgorica / the Morača river. Native XYZ zoom 17. The source imagery capture date and positional accuracy have not been independently verified. Further zoom enlarges the sampled imagery; 3 cm detail is not claimed for these zoom-17 tiles.

Licensing: public accessibility does not establish a licence. Permission for redistribution and deriving published geometry remains unconfirmed. The imagery is not covered by the application's MIT licence or the OSM attribution. No live requests to eMapa are made by the app; all sample assets are bundled locally.
