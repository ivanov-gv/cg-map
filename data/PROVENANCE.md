# Source provenance

`sources/provenance.json` inventories the actual editable layers shipped in this export.
`sources/provenance-legacy.json` preserves the predecessor's metadata; its raster-runtime fields are historical and do
not describe the current application.

The reviewed OSM extract timestamp is 2026-11-20T21:21:21Z. Display geometry remains simplified and rounded, but detail
now covers the whole city within `[19.15, 42.37, 19.34, 42.51]`, alongside countrywide major roads, rivers, canals,
attractions and the country outline. There are 43,978 buildings, 21,247 road features, 612 land features and 1,795 water
features. The land file has 605 extracted green areas, one country outline and six preserved context countries; the
water file has 1,122 polygons (including one derived ocean polygon) and 673 waterway or coastline lines. A
protected-area boundary or `place=locality` tag alone is not published as green cover. Minor stream, ditch and drain
lines are clipped to the detail box; river and canal lines stay countrywide. Unmapped or blank OSM `name` and `name:en`
tags are omitted rather than published as empty strings. The place snapshot has 1,977 entries, including named
standalone OSM points outside building footprints. No source file exceeds 40 MB; the reviewed archive is 20,985,409
bytes.

The current version-2 reviewed source bundle has thirteen GeoJSON files; the frozen version-1 bundle has the original
eight without `building-entrances.geojson` or the later settlement and border files.
`sources/building-entrances.geojson` is a read-only OSM extract of 157 tagged entrance nodes on or within one metre of a
published footprint, with 28 nearby candidates left unjoined. The refreshed `sources/buildings.geojson` carries 43,978
`building` values, 445 `building:levels`, three `building:material`, one `start_date`, 265 `name`, 142 `roof:levels`,
896 `addr:street` and 1,003 `addr:housenumber` values. Six no-number placeholders were omitted and recorded as
`nohousenumber: "yes"`. A street name joins to a road entity only when case- and whitespace-folded text matches exactly
one connected group of same-named road ways; `addr:streetEntityId` names the group's lowest way ID and
`addr:streetEntityIds` lists its members: 321 joined and 575 did not. There are 1,090 places inside a footprint and 887
outside. Counts per copied tag and entrance kind live in `sources/provenance.json` under `building_details`. No
energy-class field is published: no open OSM source in this extract supports one, and marking that gap here avoids
presenting an inferred value as a source fact.

The land/water-only refresh for issue #326 changed `sources/land.geojson` to 493,167 bytes (SHA-256
`530ad7b2f2fd05eb1a59bd9a8b688b591746f476d2b9ac7733c5da56f6799f36`) and `sources/water.geojson` to 2,097,403 bytes
(SHA-256 `edb1d55efc5c79333cc2c9355cb2ac4bf8a169e473850c56d2f444c452b7f2be`). The buildings, roads, road nodes, turn
restrictions, places, context countries, binary archive and release metadata were not regenerated by that refresh.

`sources/editor-corrections.json` appears once a reviewed changeset has been applied. Shaped by
`packages/contracts/editor-corrections.schema.json`, it records each local correction with its author, reason and full
before and after values. It is what tells a local correction from an imported upstream fact: an edited feature keeps its
upstream attribution, so read the ledger to see what changed locally. Its references are bound to one snapshot and do
not survive a re-extract, which replaces the source files.

`visibilityLevel` on each place is editorial product metadata from `sources/place-visibility-policy.json`, not an OSM
fact or a measure of significance. `places_facts_sha256` in `sources/provenance.json` covers the snapshot without that
field and still equals the hash of the pre-level snapshot. See the
[data runbook](../docs/runbooks/data.md#place-visibility-levels).

`sources/context-countries.geojson` preserves the supplied Natural Earth public-domain surrounding-country features
independently of extraction. Its features carry `ne:<code>` identities and content-hash versions; the OSM and Natural
Earth licenses remain separate.

The input PBF is not included in Git: it is an external downloadable dataset, not application source. Basemap rebuilding
from the included GeoJSON needs no PBF. This release used the 34,780,415-byte
`https://download.geofabrik.de/europe/montenegro/montenegro-latest.osm.pbf` downloaded 2026-11-21, with SHA-256
`70de54274bad52be8452852995f34280b5cbf37f2bbb207dcf56cb90410a3a5b`; the URL serves changing data. Retain the exact
snapshot outside the repository when a reproducible refresh matters.

`data/tools/extract-osm.py` is now a thin entry point to `data/tools/osm_extract/`, with one module per layer group so
later Features can add their own. A fresh complete extract, label rebuild and archive rebuild were run from the reviewed
PBF before the land/water-only refresh above. Full runs give preserved Natural Earth and ePodgorica snapshots their own
identity namespaces; `--layers` replaces only the named source files. OSM features keep the object's type, ID and
upstream version; clipped or split parts keep one identity and gain `entityPart`. See
[decision 0022](../docs/decisions/0022-extract-source-identity.md) for the constraints behind this design and
[decision 0026](../docs/decisions/0026-extracted-land-and-water-kinds.md) for the published land/water kinds, coastline
placement and ring order. It is not a full OSM semantic/cartographic pipeline; see
[architecture](../docs/system-architecture/architecture.md) before expanding coverage.

Issue #328 adds three OSM-derived read-only sources from the same reviewed PBF: `sources/settlements.geojson` has 4,009
named place nodes, `sources/border-edges.geojson` has 141 shared boundaries, and `sources/municipalities.geojson` has 26
areas (25 municipalities and one derived unassigned-water area). Together they are 2,605,818 bytes; counts, byte sizes
and SHA-256 for each file are in `sources/provenance.json`. Settlement properties copy the OSM name, population,
capital, admin level and Wikidata facts, add the `rank` and municipality code, keep the original `node/N` identity, and
sort features by `entityId` then `entityPart`. Suburb, quarter and neighbourhood nodes are limited to the whole-city
detail box; 34 place nodes outside the reviewed country outline were assigned to the nearest municipality, with a
maximum offset of 0.022884 degrees. The fallback is bounded at 0.025 degrees.

Twenty-nine edge features keep an exact `way/N` identity; the other 112 use `derived:edge-<left>-<right>-<n>` and record
their contributing OSM ways. Each shared edge is simplified once, and the municipality polygons are rebuilt in edge
traversal order from those simplified lines. Exterior sides take their authority from OSM coastline or `admin_level=2`
boundary way tags, matched to the reviewed outline within 0.00001 degrees and over 0.1% segment overlap so an endpoint
touch cannot claim an edge; a segment spanning two authorities splits where supported coverage intervals of different
authorities end and begin together. Local border names map to the reviewed context names, and Kosovo is accepted from
OSM ways because it has no Natural Earth context polygon. Territorial-water ways count as `sea`; neighbouring countries
remain context only. The country outline is the coverage authority, and water is not subtracted. Internal gaps above 100
m² and exterior water above 1,000,000 m² stay as derived `unassigned-water` areas; smaller exterior coastline residue is
absorbed into the adjacent municipality with the longest shared boundary, tie-broken by nearest distance and ISO code,
and the published gaps are then recomputed from the filled areas. Each derived area carries sorted
`neighbouringMunicipalities`, traversal-ordered `edges` and a `labelPoint`. Lake Skadar is published as
`derived:unassigned-water-1` with no `ISO3166-2`, and its shoreline and those of its municipal neighbours share one
cleaned six-decimal boundary. The reviewed sources leave 14,815 m² uncovered and 3,048 m² extra, inside the named 20,000
m² and 10,000 m² tolerances. The edge-construction rule is recorded in
[decision 0028](../docs/decisions/0028-shared-border-edges.md), and the current gap partition rule in
[decision 0029](../docs/decisions/0029-derived-unassigned-water-areas.md).

## Bus stops

`data/sources/transport-stops.geojson` contains 719 ePodgorica stops retrieved 2026-10-18 from the public stops/all
endpoint. Source IDs and coordinates are retained; only the published stopId, stopName (as name), longitude and latitude
are kept. Its independent provenance JSON records the endpoint, retrieval time, hash and count. It is a static snapshot,
separate from the OSM layers and live vehicles. No explicit reuse license was found; attribution is retained without
assigning MIT or ODbL to this source.

## Transit route patterns

transit-network.json and transit-network.provenance.json are a separately dated public ePodgorica import. They contain
stop coordinates and representative ordered route patterns with relative running times, sampled by
direction/headsign/stop count from weekday, Saturday, and Sunday trips. They are not an exhaustive timetable or service
calendar. Refresh with data/tools/import-transit.py; raw API responses are temporary and excluded from Git. Source URLs,
sampling limits, counts, hash, and license status are recorded.

## Building address labels

`building-labels.geojson` recovers published OSM house numbers for the refreshed footprints from the same reviewed
2026-11-20 extract. Its independent provenance file binds 1,207 labels to the new buildings.geojson hash and records
1,003 direct building addresses, 204 node-derived addresses and 43,923 matched footprints. Each label keeps the matched
footprint way ID and OSM way version as its source identity; real way IDs are recovered by exact rounded footprint
matching, and point addresses are joined only through unambiguous node membership or containment. No-number placeholders
and ambiguous joins are excluded. The runtime label layer is bundled for offline use. See
[building numbers](../docs/map-data/building-numbers.md).

## Relief

`contours.geojson`, `summits.geojson`, `slopes.geojson`, `ridges.geojson`, and `canyon-rims.geojson` are derived,
read-only vectors. See [ADR 0030](../docs/decisions/0030-relief-derived-read-only-sources.md).
`make -C data build-relief` rebuilds them from the local Copernicus DEM GLO-30 tiles, the reviewed OSM PBF, and the
reviewed `land.geojson` and `water.geojson`. The raster tiles, PBF and full report are external build artifacts and are
not committed; their checksums, the DEM EULA/attribution, and all output counts, bytes and SHA-256 values are recorded
in `sources/provenance.json`.

The current build has 5,411 Montenegro summits (4,541 `ok`, 417 `mismatch`, 453 `missing`), 445 contour lines across 32
levels, 152 slope polygons, 28 ridge lines, and 46 canyon-rim segments across the four river banks. The full report is
written to `.build/relief-report.json`; each summit already carries its `heightCheck`, enclosing contour, DEM facts and
reasons. OSM names and elevations are never overwritten by DEM values. A summit is checked only when both its OSM point
and its published six-decimal coordinate lie inside or on the `kind=country` boundary in `land.geojson`; the report
records 216 summit points rejected by those checks. A mismatch needs a closed contour above OSM height around the summit
or an OSM/DEM difference over 25 m. A missing OSM elevation keeps its DEM facts and is not a mismatch.

A ridge is one simplified, connected `LineString` on the shared boundary between sunlit and shaded slope polygons inside
green areas; pieces under 100 m are dropped. `summit` and `summitName` appear only on a ridge that passes within 50 m of
that summit, and the retired 400 m search radius stays in the relief provenance block as build lineage. Canyon rims keep
only banks rising at least 8 m above the river surface within 60 m of the channel, join one bank across gaps under 60 m,
then drop segments under 100 m; each remaining segment is a separate `LineString` with numbered `entityPart` values on
one derived river-bank identity. River banks are named relative to the DEM-oriented downstream centreline. The slope
builder reads the single `GREEN_KINDS` constant in `data/tools/relief/derivations.py`, aligned with the land-kind enum.
The nine configured kinds are `park`, `garden`, `woods`, `meadow`, `grass`, `scrub`, `sports`, `cemetery` and
`playground`; eight occur in the current `land.geojson` (`garden` is absent). Green kinds, discovered kinds, minimum
slope area, layer sources and the ridge/rim thresholds are recorded under `relief` in `sources/provenance.json`; relief
features carry object facts only. Derived contour, slope, ridge and rim IDs are deterministic for the pinned inputs but
are not stable across a DEM or green-area change. See [relief sources](../docs/runbooks/data.md#relief-sources) and
[data licensing](../docs/map-data/data-license.md).
