# Management

- The [management workflow](management.md) describes how work flows from the Wiki through Epics, Features, Tasks and
  Bugs to review and release.
- The [GitHub Wiki — Management page](https://github.com/ivanov-gv/cg-map/wiki/Management) is the authoritative source;
  the repository copy is for offline reference. When the two disagree, the wiki wins.
- Do not invent new process. Update the wiki first, then sync the repository copy.
